Androgens are a family of steroid hormones. 
Not just "male hormones". 

They traditionally contribute to male-pattern sexual development & effects. 
Women produce androgens too. They are involved in libido, hair growth, bone and muscle physiology, etc.

# Types of Androgens
| Androgen                  | Where mainly produced             | Potency       |                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Testosterone              | Testes, ovaries, adrenal glands   | Strong        |                                                                                                                            |
| DHT (dihydrotestosterone) | Made from testosterone in tissues | Very strong   |                                                                                                                            |
| DHEA                      | Adrenal glands                    | Weak          |                                                                                                                            |
| DHEA-S                    | Adrenal cortex (zona reticularis) | Weak          | Sulphate attachment makes it water soluble & gives it longer t1/2. <br>It's a useful marker of adrenal androgen production |
| Androstenedione           | Adrenals + ovaries/testes         | Weak/moderate |                                                                                                                            |
### DHEA-S
DHEA-S is weak androgen but it can act as a precursor for stronger androgens.
`DHEA-S (weak) → DHEA → androstenedione → testosterone → DHT (potent)`
