*It's a [[seronegative spondyloarthropathy]].* 
Aka **Reiter's Syndrome**

---
Arthritis developing after an infection elsewhere 
More common in young with [[hla-b27]]+ve. 
# Pathophysiology
Recent h/o infection: Develops within 3 weeks of initial infection, and symptoms last around 4-6 months. 
- **GU**: [[chlamydia]], [[gonorrhoea]], [[UTI]] 
- **Gastroenteritis**: Salmonella, campylobacter, Dysentery  
# Clinical Features 
Classic #triad : [[Conjunctivitis]], Urethritis, [[arthritis]] & Rash
#mnemonic : *can't see, can't pee, can't climb a tree* 
1. No fever. 
2. [[Conjunctivitis]]: Sticky, itchy eyes
3. Dysuria
	- **Circinate [[balanitis]]**: Well-demarcated, erythematous plaque with a ragged white border on the penis. Happens with chronic balanitis. 
4. Migratory oligoarthritis of lower limbs (knee, ankle) + back pain + extraarticular features.
	- [[dactylitis]], Small joints swollen.
5. May have Maculopapular waxy rash: [[keratoderma blennorrhagica]] -> palm, sole
6. Palpable cervical nodes. 
# Treatment
Sexual health clinic & rheumatology clinic. 
NSAID > Sulfasalazine, [[methotrexate]]
