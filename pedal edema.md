If someone has bad pedal edema with redness, swollen, sore legs etc, you can trial: Betnovate & frusemide for 2 weeks -> then start class 1 compression. 

# Compression Bandages
*See: [What are the compression classes for compression stockings?](https://www.medi.de/en/faq/compression-garments/compression-classes/)* 

Things to document before giving compression bandages without [[vascular ulcers|ABPI]].  
- If they have warm feet, with capillary refill. 
- Pulse palpable, no calf claudication.  
- If someone has cardiovascular history/vascular, be cautious and do ABPI.  

Level 4 compression bandage will need ABPI for sure.
