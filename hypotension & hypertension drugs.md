# Drugs with risk of Hypotension
- [[calcium channel blockers|Verapamil, Diltiazem]]
- Bromocriptine
- Levopdopa
- IMN

### Treatment: 
Fludrocortisone, 
Midodrine. Do U&e 3-4 weeks after starting/increasing. 
S/e: supine hypertension
