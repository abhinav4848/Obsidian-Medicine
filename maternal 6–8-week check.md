# Ask about Mental health problems 
Ask every woman, every time about her mental health. Never assume someone else has already asked her. Also, although a woman may have seen several healthcare professionals during her pregnancy and postnatal period, she may not have disclosed any symptoms of mental ill-health.

Identifying and addressing serious mental illness (severe depression, psychosis, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, schizoaffective disorder and postpartum psychosis) takes priority in the appointment over other areas of the consultation that can be reasonably addressed at a 
subsequent planned appointment.

Postnatal questions: 
1. Has motherhood been what you expected?
2. How was the birth? 
	1. How are you feeling about the birth?
	2. Did anything happen that may be affecting your mental health or wellbeing?
3. Explore her experiences of parenting: *How are you finding being a parent? How is the relationship with your baby?*

# Example Sequence in a GP setting. 
We'll start with you since the baby is settled. 
Was it C-section? Is it closing up fine? any discharge? Some odd twinge is expected as muscles are healing and nerve fibres are reconnecting. If pain worsens, or any discharge, wound getting wet...

Talking of down below: Any abnormal discharge/swelling?

Contraception? 
- Choice of contraception depends on ensuring body has had time to heal. I can send you reading material and when you're sure about which one you'd like, you can book an appointment to discuss that. 

How are you feeling in yourself? Support? Had advice on baby crying?

Any questions about yourself? We don't routinely scan for well healing uterus . Only do it if symptoms. Bleed/discharge/pain during intercourse. Any new symptoms, or anything you feel uncomfortable. 

Expected time for restarting periods? Exclusively breastfeeding causes no periods just because of how hormones are working at the time. However it can take 3-6 months for periods to return if using mixed feeding. Even if no periods, you can still get pregnant after 3 weeks. 

Breastfeeding? Doing it during the day then topping up with formula. 
Baby able to latch well? 

In terms of your meds and health -> Hypothyroid? Aim TSH < 2. 
-> BP. We'll check BP today and if higher than normal, we will send you home to do a longer reading and see how that goes. 

That's it for yourself, now we'll check the baby.
