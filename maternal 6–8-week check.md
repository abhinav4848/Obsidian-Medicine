# Ask about Mental health problems
Ask every woman, every time about her mental health. Never assume someone else has already asked her. Also, although a woman may have seen several healthcare professionals during her pregnancy and postnatal period, she may not have disclosed any symptoms of mental ill-health.

Identifying and addressing serious mental illness (severe depression, psychosis, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, schizoaffective disorder and postpartum psychosis) takes priority in the appointment over other areas of the consultation that can be reasonably addressed at a 
subsequent planned appointment.

Postnatal questions: 
1. Has motherhood been what you expected?
2. How was the birth? 
	1. How are you feeling about the birth?
	2. Did anything happen that may be affecting your mental health or wellbeing?
3. Explore her experiences of parenting: *How are you finding being a parent? How is the relationship with your baby?*
