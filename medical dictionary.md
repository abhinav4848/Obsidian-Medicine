# Facial plethora
Facial plethora is excessive blood flow to the face, often causing redness and swelling that can resemble a rounded "moon face". It is commonly associated with [[cushing's syndrome]], [[superior vena cava syndrome]], carcinoid syndrome, polycythemia vera, and [[acne rosacea|rosacea]].

# Frontal bossing
Frontal bossing is an unusually prominent forehead, sometimes with a heavier than normal brow ridge. It happens when the frontal bone (forehead bone) enlarges, and it may be linked with a few rare conditions such as [[acromegaly]] or [[osteomalacia|rickets]].
