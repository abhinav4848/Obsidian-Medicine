**Let the family know**
CPR is not something we would offer. 
We are not putting the burden of the decision on you. It's the clinician's decision. However we are  informing you so that we are both on the same page. 

We will not be surprised if in the next 6 months to a year, she were to die. 
So by definition she is approaching a natural end of life.

If very short time expected: 
- Might pass away in the next 24 hrs.
- Could take a turn for the worse.
# Things to write in the ReSPECT form
**Summary of relevant information for the patient:** Poor physiological reserve and poor functional status. Clinical frailty score is 6.
    
**What is important to you:** Would like to be with their neurological faculties intact
    
**Provide clinical guidance on specific interventions:** 
Symptomatic management, quality over quantity. 
Preferred place of care and death is home *If she were to know she's going to die, where would like to die. Where would her preferred place of death be?*

> Prioritise care in community unless long bone fracture, severe distress at end of life, and not managing symptoms in the community.  
> Not for CPR/ITU/NIV.
