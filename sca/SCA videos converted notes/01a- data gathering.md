# Introduction
Early open questions: 
- Weak: Tell me more about... 
- Strong: *You mentioned the pain, tell me more about the pain. Oh you mentioned work problems, tell me more about that.* 

Pain: SOCRATES
- Can you point to where the pain is?
- onset: how did it start? gradually?
- character: can you describe it? crushing/tight...
- radiation: Does it move? 
- association? (Anything else with it?): e.g headache: Is there anything else happening alongside it? What about change in your vision? Any funny sensation in your fingers? Any funny sounds in your ear? 
- Timing: we know how it started but how's it been since?
- exacerbating/relieving. 
- Severity. 

Others/Cough/palpitation: ODIPARA

# Past Medical History
Have you been diagnosed with any conditions? 
Do you have any known medical problems?
Have you been admitted to hospital?
Have you had any operations? 

h/o Asthma: have you been admitted to hospital before? Have you ever been given oral steroids? 

Don't do "MCJThreads": MI, Cancer, Jaundice, HTN, Rheumatic fever, epilepsy, asthma, diabetes, stroke.
# Drug History
Mr. x, looking at the notes, I can see from my notes that you take so and so meds... Is that right? 
Are you prescribed any regular medications? 
Do you always take these? (compliance issues)

including: 
- those prescribed (who prescribed and how long are they prescribed for)
- bought OTC
- injections (they're also meds)- insulin, injection for prostate, contraceptives as medication
- contraception (female with migraines: Can I just check if you take any contraceptive tablets or pills)
- herbal and other alternative therapy drugs? 

### Allergies
Do you have any known allergies?
if they do say they have allergy to something: What happens when you take these? (Why did this term come about?- tummy irritation vs full blown anaphylaxis)

Mr. X, when it comes to indigestion, we do prescribe some medications for this, just checking... Have you got any allergies that you know of? 

# Family History
Are there any clinical problems that run in your family. 

chest symptoms:
Mr. X, sometimes heart problems can run in families, can I just check... if anyone in your family has heart disease or heart problems. 
Brest lumps: There's a lot of breast conditions that can run in families, can I check if anyone in your family has condition related to their breast in terms of conditions / diagnosis / treatments.

# Psychosocial
Ask based on relevance. Ask in a two way approach: How is life impacting medicine and medicine impacting life. 
### Home: 
- Who else lives at home- **Support systems**
	- Are they wife / kids?
		- What does your wife think about this? Have you spoken to her about this? 
			- You can later on bring it back like *You mentioned your wife thinks this*, *You mentioned your wife already helps you with this*
	- Are you a carer for somebody. 
	- If I am to break some bad news to you right now, who have you got to discuss with when you're stressed/worried. 
- **ADLs**- Shopping/Cooking/Cleaning
	- How are you getting on with these. 
	- Do you have any carers/family
### Work:
- Certain environments can trigger a cough, like cold environments, dusty environments. Can I ask what you do for a job? 
	- has your job been impacted by this? Has there been an environment change recently? Have you taken any time off? Are you employed / self employed? 
	- If you are going to tell somebody to take time off, knowing this is useful. *You mentioned you haven't told your boss about this, do you think this is a good thing to do?*
- When it comes to headaches, there are some links to it in our life, can I ask a few things about your lifestyle. 
### Straight problem: Smoking/Alcohol causing the problem
**Smoking**:
- You mentioned this cough, one thing that can make this cough worse is smoking. Can I check if you smoke? 

**Alcohol**: 
- You mentioned some indigestion. Things like Alcohol can do it. Can I check if you drink any alcohol? 
### Reversed approach
**Other problems causing use of smoking/alcohol.**

You mentioned things have been difficult recently. Sometimes when people go through difficult times, they may turn to things they think may help them cope. 
- Things like smoking, things like alcohol. Can I check if you do any of those? 

Sometimes when people are in pain for long time, they can turn to other things which they think may help them cope....
- Things like smoking, things like alcohol even. Can I check if you do any of those?

# ICE
## Ideas
Don't ask very narrow questions like... You have back pain. What do you think it could be?

Instead ask about underlying health beliefs:
- What were your thought about these symptoms?
- Was anything going through your mind about all of this? 
- What were your own thoughts on this?
- How much are you aware about XXX?

Even for a condition which is already known to the patient, say Migraine and they've come to ask for prophylaxis: 
- How much are you aware about prophylaxis
- How much are you aware about migraine?
## Concerns
**Not**: What were you worried this could be? 
**Instead ask**: Was there anything worrying you about all of this?
