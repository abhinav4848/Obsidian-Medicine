# Teaching on bridging physical and mental health
## Title
Presenting teaching on bridging physical and mental health
## Date
10/10/2026
## Briefly describe your key learning from this event
I presented teaching on bridging physical and mental health to a fellow F2, a GP registrar and my clinical supervisor. I chose this topic because the practice is in a deprived area, with many patients experiencing unemployment, needing social care support or requiring more mental health input. We also see patients attending repeatedly (revolving door), and patients coming with medication-seeking or school-avoidance behaviour.

The teaching covered all the way from preparing for these consultations to continuity of care. I discussed points like using a CBT first approach & also taking a twin-tracks approach to physical symptoms and emotional wellbeing. During the teaching itself, there was good discussion about how to approach these patients in our practice. We also used examples of some patients that either myself, or my fellow trainees or even my supervisor have consulted with so far, and how sometimes the topics are complex enough to need MDT discussions.

Preparing the session helped me think more about how to explore the reasons behind repeated attendance and explain the link between physical and mental health without making patients feel their symptoms are being dismissed.
## Reflection: what will I maintain, improve or stop?
I think with my current communication support at PSW, using TALC, and redwhale, this was a topic worth reading up on and doing a teaching on. Past teachings in general have been clinic heavy so this was something new I wanted to explore with the team. Going forward, I will occasionally pick teaching topics that relate to patients we see in the practice.

In my own consultations, I will try to spend more time understanding what the patient is worried about and how their symptoms affect their day-to-day life (Bio-psycho-social) before moving to explanations or a plan. This is also the theme of SCA exams that I'm starting preparationfor.

I want to get better at discussing physical and mental health together, particularly when patients are focused on finding a physical cause.
## What learning needs have you identified?
- Practise using the CBT framework and twin-tracks approach in consultations, and discuss how these went with my PSW .
- Find out more about the local mental health, social prescribing and social care support available to our patients, including how to access it.
- Develop my approach to repeated attendance and medication requests, including exploring the patient's concerns and agreeing a clear plan.
