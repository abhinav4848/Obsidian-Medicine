## Balancing coverage across cases
Explicit user instruction, 15 November 2026: distribute relevant links across cases so the remaining circles are filled by the end of the period. Cases may arrive in separate messages or sessions. Never force an unrelated capability or experience group to achieve completion; prompt for suitable cases that could address remaining gaps.
- Start with the actual event and the evidence in the user's account. Read the relevant descriptors in [[gp portfolio]] and identify defensible links before considering the counts.
- Among links that fit the case, prioritise categories with more unfilled circles, taking account of proposed links for earlier cases as well as confirmed evidence. Aim to bring every category to the user's coverage goal, rather than repeatedly adding to already-filled columns.
- A category with full coverage may still be appropriate when it is the strongest or only relevant link. Quality and relevance take priority over balancing numbers.
- Record each new case's proposed allocation in [[Current coverage]], with its status. Keep this planning record separate from the confirmed dashboard snapshot. Do not double-count a case across revisions or when a new screenshot arrives.
- Briefly explain useful gap-filling choices when suggesting links. Use fewer than the maximum when the evidence does not support more.
- As gaps remain, give focused prompts for types of genuine cases or events the user may already have, or suitable future learning opportunities. Do not invent cases, actions, reflections or clinical findings.
- Do not assume a proposed case was submitted or reviewed, or that all pending allocations appear in a later screenshot. Reconcile only against available confirmation.
## Drafting conventions
These are initial working conventions, not additional preferences explicitly supplied by the user.
- Write editable, first-person drafts in clear UK English, using the supplied evidence.
- Keep completed actions, agreed plans and recommendations distinct. Do not add an ARCP outcome number, evidence of competence or personal circumstances that have not been supplied.
- Keep source records separate from the text intended for FourteenFish.
- Use the event date when known and record the draft date separately. Flag conflicting or uncertain dates outside the text intended for the form.
- Leave private notes blank unless there is a specific reason or user request to include them.
