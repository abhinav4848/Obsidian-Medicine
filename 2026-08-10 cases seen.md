### Cubital tunnel syndrome
Ulnar nerve affected in the hand. 
##### Lumbrical supply
1st & 2nd lumbricals (lateral two) → [[upper limb ortho problems#Nerve injuries linked|median nerve]]
3rd & 4th lumbricals (medial two) → Deep branch of the ulnar nerve

| Site                   | Key features                                                          |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cubital tunnel (elbow) | Ulnar symptoms in hand; often worse with elbow flexion                |
| Guyon's canal (wrist)  | Ulnar hand symptoms but dorsal ulnar hand sensation is usually spared |
**Exam pearl**: If the dorsal sensation of the little finger is affected, think ulnar nerve compression proximal to the wrist, e.g. at the elbow.

Ulnar nerve compression around the elbow is called cubital tunnel syndrome.
