Common sun-induced scaly or hyperkeratotic lesion which are #pre-malignant. (Actinic keratoses & [[cancer- skin|bowen's disease]] can become SCC, also [[cancer- skin|leukoplakia]]).

Characterized by multifocal, hyperpigmented or scaly lesions, usually brown with a scaly base occurring on the head/neck/forearms/hands.
Not painful. 

🚩Patients on immunosuppression (Transplant) -> Urgent derm referral for any new or growing skin lesions. 
Also note patient on meds like Tacrolimus
## Treatment
- Prevent further risk with sunscreen, sun avoidance
- Flurouracil (Efudix) cream 2-3 week course -> but skin gets inflamed -> give topical hydrocortisone to help with inflammation but only after 2 weeks of the cream.
- Topical diclofenac (solareze) may be used in mild AK/Imiquimod/Cryotherapy/curettage
